    What is 432 Hz?

    Almost all modern music is tuned with the note A at 440 Hz, the international standard (ISO 16). Tuning to 432 Hz lowers every note by 1.8%, about 32 cents: less than a third of a semitone.

    What this tool does: it lowers the pitch of the whole song by that amount and corrects the speed so it lasts exactly as long. Nothing else changes.

    To be honest: many people hear the result as slightly warmer and softer, but there is no solid scientific evidence that 432 Hz has special effects on health or wellbeing. It's a matter of taste. Use the comparison and decide for yourself.

    If the original song wasn't tuned to 440 Hz, the result won't be exactly 432 Hz either.

    Conversion happens in your browser. YouTube links are fetched through an external service (Apify) and the audio is deleted there as soon as it reaches you. Cookie-free analytics.
